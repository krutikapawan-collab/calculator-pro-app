## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Calculator 1+ is the ultimate all-in-one money tool for everyday calculations, tax planning, loan management, currency conversion, and tracking your udhaar-jama (credit-debit) ledger. Designed with a clean, modern interface, it brings five powerful financial tools together in one lightweight app — no ads, no sign-up, completely free.

★ CALCULATOR
A full-featured calculator with support for parentheses, percentages, sign toggling, and backspace. Get instant results as you type with a large, easy-to-read display. Perfect for quick everyday math.

★ GST / TAX CALCULATOR
Add or remove GST from any amount with a single tap. Supports all Indian GST slabs (3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and automatically breaks down CGST and SGST components. Ideal for shopkeepers, freelancers, and small business owners.

★ LOAN EMI CALCULATOR
Calculate your monthly EMI for home, car, or personal loans. Adjust loan amount, interest rate, and tenure with intuitive sliders. See the total interest, total payable amount, and a visual principal-vs-interest breakdown instantly.

★ CURRENCY CONVERTER
Convert between 12 major world currencies with live exchange rates. Works offline using cached rates. Includes USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, AUD, CAD, CNY, AED, SGD, SAR, and PKR. Perfect for travelers and international transactions.

★ PATTHI (LEDGER BOOK)
Keep track of your udhaar-jama (credit-debit) transactions with anyone. Add entries with a name, amount, and optional note. The app automatically calculates the net balance so you always know who owes what. Your ledger entries are saved securely in the cloud and synced across your devices.
